## Scope

The only surface: a Portuguese shopping list. Visitor mode is Operate.

## Audience and job

One person, often with the phone in one hand, adding what they still need to buy and changing quantities without opening another screen.

## Task

Add an item by typing its name and its quantity, edit its name, step the quantity, drag a line into a new place, or remove it. The list remains in this browser.

## Constraints

Quantity is typed beside the item name when the line is added, with a numeric keyboard, and changed afterward with plus above the number and minus below it. Lines reorder from the grip on the left. Items stay far enough apart that those controls are not easy to hit by mistake. No accounts, prices, categories, or check-off.

## Direction

Brief-pinned market list, seed brief-pinned-lista. Sand ground, deep-moss title plate, terracotta only on Adicionar, a moss stepper on the right of each badge. The memorable moment is the quantity rolling inside that stepper as a line unfolds or collapses.
